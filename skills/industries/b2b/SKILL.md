[INDUSTRY CAUTIONS: SYNTHETIC ONLY]
Audience: Professional service buyers. Needs: Scope, evidence, delivery expectations. Tone: Precise, restrained, no professional guarantees.
Never invent brand facts, evidence, credentials or outcomes. Unsupported medical,
financial, housing or professional claims require human review, not invented facts.
These US-reference cautions are not jurisdictional compliance approval.
